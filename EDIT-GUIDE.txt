HOW TO EDIT YOUR SITE (no AI or coding needed)

YOUR FILES
  index.html   All the words on the page, in order from top to bottom.
  settings.js  Your giving link, email, video, partner count.
  style.css    Colors and fonts (see the top of the file).
  photo.jpg    Your photo (add this yourself).

COMMON CHANGES
  Change any words:     Open index.html and edit the text between > and <.
                        Look for [brackets]: those are placeholders to replace.
  Add your video:       In settings.js, paste the YouTube ID into videoId.
  Update partner count: In settings.js, change partnersNow.
  Change the look:      At the top of style.css, change a #hex color or a font name.
  Giving form:          Gift amounts are set inside your Raisely account, not here.
                        The form shows via giveEmbedUrl in settings.js.
                        If it ever looks wrong, set giveEmbedUrl to "" and the page
                        will still show the link to your giving page.

HOW TO PUBLISH A CHANGE (easiest way)
  1. Go to your repository on github.com and click the file.
  2. Click the pencil icon, make your edit, click "Commit changes".
  3. Cloudflare Pages updates your live site automatically within a minute or two.

If you break something, GitHub keeps every old version. Open the file's History and restore the earlier one.
