We couldn't stay the same after meeting Jesus. (It's probably wouldn't be possible even if we tried).

God changed everything for us. Though we grew up knowing the truth of the gospel, when we personally met the love of Jesus through moments in His presence, we saw that He was worth giving everything for. During this season, we also began building relationship with EveryHeart by attending local gatherings and eagerly gleaning from the experience of their leadership.

In 2021, God called us into ministry. We had no experience, just a burning conviction that the same love we had encountered was for a generation. Through worship nights, evangelism outreaches, and media initiatives, we began mobilizing our generation to respond to this love that changed our lives.

In November of 2023, God spoke to Ben about joining EveryHeart as missionaries. After confirmation through Claire in January 2024, we dropped everything to pursue this calling full-time. After attending a 3 month ministry school, we packed our life belongings in a storage unit and headed on the road for 10 weeks to preach the gospel across America.

We are continuing to run with EveryHeart for the foreseeable future. Whether we are building the infrastructure of the ministry through media, administration, or preaching the gospel on the streets, we are honored to see the gospel go forth in a generation in our day-to-day lives.
